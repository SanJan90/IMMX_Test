Who is AFTC

A specialist manufacturer of acrylic foam tapes.

AFTC® Group manufactures double-sided viscoelastic 100% acrylic foam tapes under its registered trademark SilverTape™. These tapes replace traditional bonding methods — absorbing thermal expansion, dampening vibration and distributing stress across the entire bond line.

From automotive body panels to solar frames and architectural façades, our tapes deliver invisible, permanent joins that outperform mechanical fasteners in both strength and finish.

Why acrylic foam tape

One bond line. Every advantage.

Unlike rivets or welds that concentrate stress at single points, SilverTape™ distributes load across the entire joint — sealing, damping and bonding in a single step. The result is stronger, lighter and cleaner than mechanical fastening.
